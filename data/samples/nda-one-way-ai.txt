NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of March 3, 2026, by and between Acme Industries, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Acme"), and Vertex Outsourcing LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Counterparty").

The parties wish to evaluate a potential vendor relationship for an outsourcing proposal (the "Purpose").

1. Purpose

Each party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose.

2. Confidentiality

Acme shall keep Counterparty's Confidential Information secret. Counterparty undertakes no confidentiality obligation to Acme.

3. Term

The confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years from the date of each disclosure.

4. Return or destruction

Upon written request, the receiving party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and all copies, and shall retain nothing.

5. Compelled disclosure

If a party is compelled by law, regulation, or legal process to disclose Confidential Information, it shall disclose only what is legally required. Before any such disclosure, and to the extent legally permitted, the receiving party shall give the disclosing party prompt written notice so that a protective order may be sought.

6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules.

7. Personal data

Confidential Information does not include personal data, and neither party may disclose personal data under this Agreement unless a data processing addendum is signed first.

8. Model training

Counterparty may use Acme's Confidential Information to train and fine-tune machine learning models.
